# Platform Conventions

Per-platform writing rules. Claims are marked **[evidenced]** where a study or the platform itself backs them, and **[practice]** where it is convention that practitioners agree on but nobody has measured properly. Treat practice items as defaults to test, not laws.

---

## LinkedIn

**Hard limit:** 3,000 characters including spaces. Longer content becomes an article.

**Target length:** 1,800–2,800 characters (roughly 300–600 words). **[evidenced]** — a 2025 analysis found 100–300-word posts averaged 4.2% engagement, 300–800 words 5.8%, and 800–1,500 words 6.1%; a July 2026 study of 895 posts reached the same conclusion. Longer wins *when it holds attention*. Optimise for whether the reader finishes, not for brevity.

**The fold:** roughly the first 140 characters show before "see more" (device-dependent, so treat as an estimate). LinkedIn has also stated the first line feeds the post URL and search indexing, so it carries discovery weight beyond the click. **[evidenced]**

**Structure:**

```
Hook                    1–2 lines, contrarian claim or surprising fact
Problem / context       2–4 short paragraphs, what most people get wrong
Named framework         a titled list of 3–5 items, label + short explanation
Action                  1–3 paragraphs, concrete next moves
Engagement question     one question, at the end
```

**Whitespace:** paragraphs of 1–3 sentences, blank line between each. Dense blocks fail. **[evidenced]** — consistent with LinkedIn's own engagement guidance.

**Hashtags:** 1–3, specific, multiword and capitalised, at the end. **[evidenced]** — 2024 Statista analysis found 1–3 produced the highest average likes and led on impressions, clicks, shares and comments; a 2025 peer-reviewed study of 991 posts confirmed hashtags lift reactions.

**Emoji:** 2–3 distinct types maximum, at the start of list items, never inline in prose. **[practice]**

**Never:** em-dashes, "In today's fast-paced world", hashtags in the hook, markdown (LinkedIn renders none of it — export plain text).

## Instagram

**Hard limit:** 2,200 characters. About 125 show before "more". **[practice]**

**Target length:** under ~30 words for most accounts. **[evidenced]** — a Socialinsider study of 9.1 million posts across 82,952 business accounts found engagement generally declined as captions lengthened. This is directional: storytelling accounts legitimately run longer when the content earns it.

**First line:** self-contained, curiosity- or question-led. It has to work with no context and no image.

**CTA:** put the high-priority ask near the top rather than only at the end **[evidenced]** — most readers never expand. A short second CTA at the end catches those who do. Ask for one specific behaviour: comment an answer, save it, tag someone, use the bio link. Not "engage".

**Hashtags:** 3–5 tight and relevant. **[contested]** — Shopify recommends 3–5, a HubSpot survey found marketers most often use 3–11, and older HubSpot data claimed 11+ performed best. The findings genuinely conflict. Start at 3–5 and test against your own reach and saves. Instagram has confirmed that caption keywords and hashtags feed Search, so relevance beats volume.

**Carousels:** produce slide copy as well as the caption. Carousels earn more saves than single images.

```
Slide 1    the hook, large text, readable at thumbnail size
Slides 2-7 one idea per slide, roughly 15 words each
Last slide the CTA
```

**Emoji:** audience-dependent, no cross-platform norm. Test in your niche.

## X / Twitter

**Hard limit:** 280 characters, more with Premium.

**Target length:** 100–200 characters. **[evidenced]** — an 87,000-post analysis (May 2026) found 100–200 chars had the highest engagement rate at 1.09%, while sub-100-char posts drew the most impressions but a lower 0.68% engagement rate.

**Threads outperform single posts:** 1.89% engagement versus 0.67% for text-only. **[evidenced]** Use them for connected frameworks, case studies and stories — never to pad a single idea.

```
Post 1   standalone hook, states the outcome the reader gets
Post 2-n one idea each, numbered or cued
Last     the takeaway plus one CTA
```

**Ranking:** X's open-source algorithm weights replies heavily, and especially conversations where the author replies back. So end on a question you intend to answer. **[evidenced]** — from the published `xai-org/x-algorithm` source.

**Hashtags:** unnecessary for reach. **[practice]**

**Emoji:** fine inline, no strong norm against.

## Threads (Meta)

500 characters. Conversational, more personal than LinkedIn, closer to X in norms. Fewer hashtags than Instagram. Maximum 5 unique URLs per post, enforced since December 2025. A profile can publish 250 posts per 24 hours via the API.

## TikTok / Reels

**The first 3 seconds are the whole game.** Instagram uses View Rate — the share of viewers still watching at 3 seconds — as a primary distribution signal. **[evidenced]** TikTok's equivalent window is about 6 seconds.

**Hook type:** curiosity gaps and urgency outperform direct-address and instructional openings. **[evidenced]** — a preprint study of 46,605 TikTok hooks. Optimal hook length varies a lot by niche, so there is no universal number.

A VidMob analysis of 1,678 TikTok ads across 7.3 billion impressions found strong 6-second hooks doubled engagement and lifted purchase intent 43%, that everyday creators were 1.7× more likely to hook viewers than celebrities, and that direct-to-camera footage improved 2-second view-through by 14%. **[evidenced, but paid-ad data]** — organic behaviour may differ.

**Script format:**

```
0-1s    [visual]         movement, or a striking result on screen
1-3s    [spoken]         the promise or problem, stated plainly
        [on-screen text] the same claim in writing
3-20s   [beats]          rapid proof, one point per beat
end     [payoff]         plus a specific comment prompt
```

Write spoken lines and on-screen text separately. They do different jobs, and on-screen text is read by people watching with sound off.

**Captions are search.** TikTok reported billions of daily searches in 2025, up more than 40% year on year, and ships Creator Search Insights specifically to surface search demand. **[evidenced]** Put the words someone would actually search into the caption and the on-screen text.

**Hashtags:** 5 or fewer, accurate and topical. Skip generic ones like #fyp. **[practice]** — TikTok's own 2025 guidance; treat hashtags as indexing rather than a reach hack.
